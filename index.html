The Prasanthi Charm

An authentic lakeside sanctuary designed for intimate living.

Nestled along the rippling shores of Ashtamudi Lake in Kollam, Prasanthi Farm House combines traditional Kerala heritage cottage architecture with the calming expanse of backwater nature.

Featuring signature clay-tiled roofs, deep forest green verandas (Poomukham) with traditional seating ledges, lantern light ambience, and a pristine grass lawn stretching straight to the water’s edge—it is a private paradise where you can unwind in complete peace.
